# Information Security Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Information Security, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees, interns and contractors.

## 1. Data classification
All company information falls into one of four classes:
- Public: approved for public release, such as marketing material.
- Internal: for use inside Kestrel Labs, such as internal announcements.
- Confidential: business information, such as contracts, financials and source code.
- Restricted: customer personal data and credentials. Restricted data must never be stored on laptops, must stay in approved production systems, and must never be sent by email.

## 2. Handling information
- Company information must not be sent to personal email accounts or stored on personal cloud drives.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- A clean desk is required: no confidential documents may be left on desks at the end of the day.

## 3. Generative AI tools
- Kestrel Assist is the only approved AI tool for Confidential information.
- Public AI tools may be used only with Public or Internal information. Confidential and Restricted information must never be entered into public AI tools.

## 4. Security incidents
- Any security incident, such as a suspected breach, malware infection or lost device, must be reported to security@kestrellabs.example within 1 hour of discovery.
- Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report Phishing" button in the email client. Do not click links or open attachments in them.

## 5. Training
- New joiners must complete information security training within 15 days of joining.
- All employees must complete the annual information security refresher by 31 March each year. Accounts of employees who miss the deadline are restricted until the training is completed.

## 6. Violations
Violations of this policy are handled under the disciplinary process in the Code of Conduct.
