# Intern Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All interns, for the duration of their internship. Full-time employees are covered by the Leave Policy for Employees, not this policy.

## 1. Casual leave for interns
- Interns earn 1 day of casual leave for every completed month of internship. For example, an intern on a 6-month internship can earn up to 6 casual leaves in total.
- Casual leave is credited at the end of each completed month, so an intern cannot take casual leave in the first month.
- A maximum of 2 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at one time.

## 2. Sick leave for interns
- Interns get a total of 3 days of sick leave for the entire internship, not per month.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 1 day.

## 3. Leave types not available to interns
- Interns do not earn earned leave.
- Interns are not eligible for marriage leave or compensatory off.
- Interns are entitled to the fixed public holidays of their office location, but not to optional holidays. See the Holiday Calendar 2026.

## 4. Academic leave
Interns may take up to 5 days of academic leave during the internship for university examinations or viva. Academic leave is unpaid and requires a letter from the college. It must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.

## 5. Approval
All intern leave is approved first by the intern's mentor and then by the HR internship coordinator on KestrelHub.

## 6. Lapse and encashment
All unused intern leave lapses on the last day of the internship. Intern leave is never encashed and is not converted into stipend.

## 7. Unapproved absence
If an intern is absent without approved leave for more than 3 days in a month, the stipend for those days is deducted pro-rata. Unapproved absence of more than 5 consecutive working days may lead to early termination of the internship under the Internship Program Rules.
