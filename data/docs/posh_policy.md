# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources and the Internal Committee, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees, interns, contractors, consultants and visitors, at the workplace and at work-related events, offsites and business travel.

## 1. Purpose
Kestrel Labs has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. This policy follows the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, and also covers complaints from employees of any gender.

## 2. Internal Committee (IC)
- The Internal Committee has 4 members.
- The Presiding Officer is a senior woman employee.
- At least half of the members are women.
- One member is an external member from an NGO or a legal background.
- IC members serve a term of 3 years.

## 3. Filing a complaint
- A complaint should be filed in writing with the IC at ic@kestrellabs.example within 3 months of the incident, or of the last incident in a series.
- The IC may extend this time limit by a further 3 months if there were valid reasons for the delay.

## 4. Inquiry
- The IC completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving the complaint.
- The IC shares its findings and recommendations within 10 days of completing the inquiry.
- During the inquiry, the complainant may ask for interim relief, such as a transfer, a change of reporting manager, or paid leave of up to 3 months.

## 5. Confidentiality and retaliation
The identity of the complainant, the respondent and the witnesses, and the details of the inquiry, are kept confidential. Retaliation against a complainant or witness is serious misconduct.

## 6. Training
All employees and interns must complete POSH awareness training within 15 days of joining and every year after that.
