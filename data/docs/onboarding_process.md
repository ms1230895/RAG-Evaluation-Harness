# Onboarding Process

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All new full-time employees. Interns follow the same process except where stated.

## 1. Before joining
- New joiners must upload the following on KestrelHub at least 3 working days before the joining date: PAN card, Aadhaar card, educational certificates, relieving letter from the previous employer (not required for freshers or interns), and bank account details.
- IT creates the company email account and KestrelHub login before the joining date and sends the login details to the joiner's personal email.
- A background verification is started by a third-party agency after the offer is accepted. It must be completed within 30 days of joining. The offer may be withdrawn if verification fails.

## 2. Day 1
- Orientation starts at 9:30 AM at the Bengaluru (Indiranagar) office or the Pune (Baner) office.
- The new joiner receives a company laptop on Day 1. See the Device and BYOD Policy.
- Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 30 days. Interns are assigned a mentor instead.
- The access ID card is issued within 3 working days. A temporary visitor badge is used until then.

## 3. Mandatory steps after joining
- Read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct on KestrelHub within 7 days of joining.
- Complete the information security training within 15 days of joining.
- Complete the POSH (prevention of sexual harassment) awareness training within 15 days of joining.
- Set up multi-factor authentication on Day 1, as required by the IT Access Policy.
- Submit the PF nomination form within the first month.

## 4. Check-ins
The reporting manager holds structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days after joining. The 90-day check-in feeds into the probation review described in the Probation and Confirmation Policy.
