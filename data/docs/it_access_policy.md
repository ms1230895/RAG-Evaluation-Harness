# IT Access Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: IT Operations, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees, interns and contractors who use Kestrel Labs systems.

## 1. Accounts
- IT creates the company email and KestrelHub accounts before the joining date.
- Every user has one named account. Shared accounts and shared passwords are prohibited.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, KestrelHub, the VPN and all internal tools, and must be set up on Day 1.

## 2. Passwords
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. They must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- Locked accounts can be unlocked through the self-service password reset page.

## 3. Requesting access
- Access to tools, repositories and shared drives is requested through a ticket on KestrelDesk, the IT service desk, and must be approved by the reporting manager.
- Access to production systems requires additional approval from the Engineering Manager and the Information Security team. Production access is valid for 90 days and must then be re-certified.
- Interns are never granted production access.

## 4. Administrator rights
Users do not have administrator rights on company laptops by default. Temporary administrator rights for up to 24 hours can be requested through a KestrelDesk ticket.

## 5. Remote access
All internal systems must be accessed over the company VPN when outside the office network.

## 6. Access reviews
Managers review their team members' access every quarter. Access that is no longer needed is removed.

## 7. Leaving the company
All accounts are deactivated by 6:00 PM on the employee's last working day.

## 8. IT helpdesk
The IT helpdesk is available at extension 4357 or through KestrelDesk, from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST on working days.
