# Health Insurance and Benefits Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Human Resources (Benefits), Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees. Interns are covered only by the group personal accident policy (section 5).

## 1. Group medical insurance
- Every full-time employee is covered by group medical insurance from the date of joining.
- The sum insured is ₹5,00,000 per family per policy year, on a floater basis.
- The family covered is the employee, their spouse and up to 2 children.
- Pre-existing conditions are covered from day 1. There is no waiting period.
- Room rent is capped at 2% of the sum insured per day (₹10,000 per day).
- Maternity expenses are covered up to ₹75,000 per delivery, within the sum insured.
- Outpatient (OPD) consultations and medicines are not covered.
- The premium for the employee, spouse and children is paid by the company.

## 2. Parents cover
Employees can add their parents or parents-in-law under a separate parents cover with a sum insured of ₹3,00,000. The premium for parents cover is paid by the employee through salary deduction.

## 3. Adding dependants
- A newborn child must be added on KestrelHub within 30 days of birth.
- A spouse must be added within 30 days of the date of marriage.
- Outside these windows, dependants can be added only at the annual renewal on 1 April.

## 4. Claims
Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals through CarePoint TPA, the third-party administrator. For non-network hospitals, reimbursement claims must be submitted to CarePoint TPA within 30 days of discharge.

## 5. Life and accident cover
- Group term life insurance: 3 times the employee's annual fixed CTC.
- Group personal accident insurance: 3 times the employee's annual fixed CTC.
- Interns are covered by group personal accident insurance of ₹10,00,000. Interns are not covered by medical insurance or term life insurance.

## 6. Annual health check-up
Employees aged 35 and above can take one free annual health check-up at a partner diagnostic center.
