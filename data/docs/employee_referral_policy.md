# Employee Referral Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Talent Acquisition, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees.

## 1. How to refer
Referrals are submitted through the referral page on KestrelHub with the candidate's resume. A referral stays valid for 6 months from the date of submission.

## 2. Referral bonus
- ₹50,000 for a successful referral into a role in bands B1 to B3.
- ₹1,00,000 for a successful referral into a role in band B4 or above.

## 3. When the bonus is paid
- The bonus is paid after the referred candidate completes 90 days of employment, in the next payroll.
- The referring employee must still be employed by Kestrel Labs on the payout date. No bonus is paid if the referrer has left or is serving notice.

## 4. Who is not eligible
- Members of the Talent Acquisition and HR teams.
- Hiring managers, for roles in their own team.
- Interns. Interns may refer candidates, but they do not receive a referral bonus.

## 5. Duplicate referrals
If two employees refer the same candidate, the referral submitted first on KestrelHub is counted. Candidates who are already in the hiring system through any other channel are not counted as referrals.

## 6. Former employees
Referring a former employee who left Kestrel Labs within the last 12 months does not earn a referral bonus.
