# Probation and Confirmation Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All new full-time employees. Interns do not have a probation period.

## 1. Probation period
- Employees in bands B1 to B3 have a probation period of 6 months from the date of joining.
- Employees in bands B4 to B6 have a probation period of 3 months.

## 2. Probation review
- The reporting manager completes a probation review on KestrelHub in the last month of probation (month 5 for a 6-month probation, month 3 for a 3-month probation).
- Possible outcomes: confirm, extend, or end employment.
- Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months. A second extension is not allowed.
- A confirmation letter is issued within 7 working days after a positive review.

## 3. Restrictions during probation
While on probation, an employee:
- cannot take earned leave, although earned leave accrues from the date of joining;
- is not eligible for a salary advance;
- cannot apply for an internal transfer to another team;
- cannot use the learning and development budget.

Casual leave, sick leave and parental leave are available during probation.

## 4. Notice period during probation
The notice period during probation is 15 days, on either side. After confirmation, the notice periods in the Resignation and Exit Policy apply.

## 5. Interns converting to full-time
An intern who receives a pre-placement offer and joins as a full-time employee serves a probation period of 3 months instead of 6, regardless of band.
