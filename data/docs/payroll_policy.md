# Payroll Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Finance and Payroll, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees. Intern stipends are covered in section 8.

## 1. Pay date
Salary is credited to the employee's registered bank account on the last working day of each month. If the last day of the month is a weekend or a company holiday, salary is credited on the preceding working day.

## 2. Payroll cut-off
The payroll cut-off date is the 20th of each month. Attendance, leave, reimbursement and joining changes approved after the 20th are processed in the following month's payroll. Employees who join after the 20th receive their first month's salary together with the next month's payroll.

## 3. Payslips
Payslips are published on KestrelHub by the 5th of the following month.

## 4. Salary structure
- Basic salary is 40% of the fixed annual CTC.
- House Rent Allowance (HRA) is 50% of basic salary for employees based in Bengaluru and Pune.
- The remaining fixed pay is paid as a special allowance.
- Bands run from B1 (Associate) to B6 (Director).

## 5. Provident Fund (PF) and gratuity
- The employee contributes 12% of basic salary to the Provident Fund and the company contributes a matching 12%.
- By default, PF is calculated on basic salary capped at ₹15,000 per month. Employees can opt to contribute on their full basic salary by informing payroll in the first month of employment.
- Gratuity is payable to employees who complete 5 years of continuous service, calculated as 15/26 × last drawn monthly basic salary × completed years of service.

## 6. Income tax
- Investment declarations for the financial year must be submitted on KestrelHub by 30 April.
- Investment proofs must be submitted by 31 January. If proofs are not submitted, tax is recalculated without the declared investments for the remaining months.
- Form 16 is issued by 15 June every year.

## 7. Salary advance
Confirmed employees may request a salary advance of up to 1 month's net salary, once in any 12-month period. The advance is interest-free and is recovered in 6 equal monthly instalments. Employees on probation are not eligible.

## 8. Intern stipends
Interns are paid a fixed monthly stipend on the same pay date as employees. No PF is deducted from intern stipends. Stipend amounts are listed in the Internship Program Rules.

## 9. Payroll queries
Send payroll queries to payroll@kestrellabs.example. Queries are resolved within 5 working days.
