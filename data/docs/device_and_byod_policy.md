# Device and BYOD Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: IT Operations, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees and interns.

## 1. Company laptops
- Engineering, data and design roles receive a MacBook Pro. All other roles receive a Lenovo ThinkPad.
- Laptops are refreshed every 4 years, or earlier if IT certifies the device as faulty.
- Company laptops are managed by IT. Users must not remove or disable the device management, antivirus or disk encryption software.
- USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops.

## 2. Personal use
Reasonable personal use of the company laptop is allowed. Installing pirated software, torrent clients or games is not allowed.

## 3. Bring your own device (BYOD)
- Personal mobile phones may be used for company email and chat only after they are enrolled in the company mobile device management (MDM) app.
- Personal laptops and personal tablets must not be used to access any company system.
- Interns may not use any personal device, including phones, for company work.

## 4. Lost or stolen devices
- A lost or stolen company laptop or MDM-enrolled phone must be reported to IT and to security@kestrellabs.example within 24 hours.
- For theft, a police report (FIR) must be filed and a copy shared with IT.
- If a laptop is lost because of negligence, the employee pays 25% of the depreciated value of the laptop for the first loss and 100% for any further loss.

## 5. Returning devices
Company laptops, chargers and accessories must be returned on or before the last working day. The depreciated value of any device not returned is deducted from the full and final settlement.
