# Learning and Development Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Human Resources (Learning and Development), Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All confirmed full-time employees.

## 1. Annual learning budget
- Each confirmed employee has a learning budget of ₹50,000 per financial year (1 April to 31 March).
- The budget can be used for online courses, books, certifications and conferences related to the employee's current or planned role.
- Unused budget does not carry forward to the next financial year.
- Employees on probation cannot use the learning budget. Interns are not eligible for the budget, but get access to the internal learning platform.

## 2. Certifications
- Certification exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam.
- The fee for one failed attempt per certification is also reimbursed.

## 3. Expensive programs and service agreement
- Any single course or program costing more than ₹1,00,000 needs approval from the department head and is paid directly by the company.
- The employee must sign a 12-month service agreement for such programs. If the employee leaves within 12 months of completing the program, they repay the cost pro-rata for the months not served.

## 4. Conferences
Employees may attend 1 external conference per financial year with manager approval. Conference fees, travel and stay count towards the annual learning budget. Travel is booked under the Travel Policy.

## 5. Exam leave
Confirmed employees get up to 3 days of paid exam leave per financial year for approved certification exams.

## 6. How to claim
Learning expenses are claimed on KestrelHub under the "Learning" category, with the receipt and course details, and follow the approval rules in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.
