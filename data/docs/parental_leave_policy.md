# Parental Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees, including those on probation. Interns are not covered by this policy.

## 1. Maternity leave
- Eligible employees get 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for each of their first two children.
- For the third child onwards, paid maternity leave is 12 weeks.
- To be eligible, the employee must have worked at Kestrel Labs for at least 80 days in the 12 months immediately before the expected date of delivery.
- Up to 8 weeks of maternity leave can be taken before the expected date of delivery.
- In case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, the employee gets 6 weeks of paid leave from the date of the event.

## 2. Adoption and surrogacy
- An employee who adopts a child below 3 months of age gets 12 weeks of paid adoption leave from the date the child is handed over.
- A commissioning mother (surrogacy) gets 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over.

## 3. Paternity leave
- Male employees and non-birthing partners get 10 working days of paid paternity leave for the birth or adoption of a child.
- Paternity leave must be taken within 3 months of the date of birth or adoption.
- It can be taken in a maximum of 2 blocks.

## 4. Return to work
- After maternity leave, an employee may work from home for up to 3 months, with the approval of the reporting manager.
- Employees returning from maternity leave cannot be rated 1 (Needs Improvement) for the review cycle in which the leave was taken, on the basis of the leave period.

## 5. Creche allowance
Employees who are parents of children up to 4 years of age can claim a creche allowance of up to ₹4,000 per month per family, on submission of the creche fee receipt.

## 6. How to apply
Apply on KestrelHub at least 8 weeks before the planned start of maternity leave, or 2 weeks before paternity leave, and upload the medical certificate or adoption documents.
