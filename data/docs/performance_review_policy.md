# Performance Review Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All confirmed full-time employees. Interns are reviewed under the Internship Program Rules.

## 1. Review cycle
- The performance year runs from 1 April to 31 March.
- A mid-year check-in takes place in October. It is a feedback conversation and does not produce a rating.
- Self-appraisals for the annual review must be submitted on KestrelHub by 15 April.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees who join on or after 1 October are not rated in that performance year. Their first rating is in the next cycle.

## 3. Rating scale
- 5: Outstanding
- 4: Exceeds expectations
- 3: Meets expectations
- 2: Partially meets expectations
- 1: Needs improvement

Ratings are calibrated across each department before they are final.

## 4. Salary increments
Salary increments are effective from 1 July. They are paid from the July salary, together with any arrears.

## 5. Variable pay
- Variable pay is paid once a year with the July salary.
- The payout depends on a company performance multiplier and an individual rating multiplier.
- Employees rated 1 receive no variable pay.

## 6. Promotions
To be considered for promotion, an employee must have spent at least 18 months in their current band and must have a rating of 4 or 5 in the most recent cycle.

## 7. Performance improvement plan (PIP)
Employees rated 1 are placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan with clear goals agreed with their manager. If the goals are not met, employment may be ended.
