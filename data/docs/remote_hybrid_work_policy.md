# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees and interns.

## 1. Hybrid work as the default
- Full-time employees work from the office at least 3 days per week.
- Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are anchor days. Teams should plan office days on anchor days.
- Interns must work from the office at least 4 days per week.

## 2. Fully remote work
- Fully remote work is allowed only with approval from the department's Vice President.
- Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 6 months and can be withdrawn with 30 days' notice.

## 3. Working from another city
- Employees may work from another city in India for up to 30 working days per calendar year, with manager approval.
- Working from outside India is not allowed without written approval from HR and the Information Security team, because of tax and data protection rules.

## 4. Home setup support
- Internet: employees on hybrid or remote work can claim up to ₹1,000 per month for home internet, as set out in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.
- Ergonomic chair: confirmed employees can claim a one-time reimbursement of up to ₹8,000 for an ergonomic chair.

## 5. Availability and security
- Working hours and core hours are the same at home and in the office. See the Attendance and Working Hours Policy.
- Company systems must be accessed only from the company laptop and over the company VPN, as set out in the IT Access Policy.
- Confidential calls must not be taken in public places such as cafes or co-working spaces.
