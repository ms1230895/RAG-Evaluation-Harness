# Holiday Calendar 2026

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees and interns at the Bengaluru (Indiranagar) and Pune (Baner) offices.

## 1. Fixed holidays
Each office has 12 fixed paid holidays in 2026. Ten are common to both offices and two are location-specific.

Common fixed holidays (both offices):
1. New Year's Day: Thursday, 1 January 2026
2. Republic Day: Monday, 26 January 2026
3. Holi: Wednesday, 4 March 2026
4. Ugadi / Gudi Padwa: Thursday, 19 March 2026
5. Good Friday: Friday, 3 April 2026
6. Independence Day: Saturday, 15 August 2026
7. Ganesh Chaturthi: Monday, 14 September 2026
8. Gandhi Jayanti: Friday, 2 October 2026
9. Dussehra (Vijayadashami): Tuesday, 20 October 2026
10. Christmas: Friday, 25 December 2026

Location-specific fixed holidays:
- Bengaluru office: Diwali (Balipadyami) on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, and Karnataka Rajyotsava on Sunday, 1 November 2026.
- Pune office: Maharashtra Day on Friday, 1 May 2026, and Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) on Monday, 9 November 2026.

## 2. Holidays falling on a weekend
When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, no substitute holiday is given. In 2026 this applies to Independence Day (Saturday, 15 August) and, for the Bengaluru office, Karnataka Rajyotsava (Sunday, 1 November).

## 3. Optional holidays
Employees may choose 2 optional holidays in 2026 from the list below. Optional holidays must be applied for on KestrelHub at least 7 days in advance. Unused optional holidays lapse on 31 December and do not convert into leave.

- Makar Sankranti: Wednesday, 14 January 2026
- Eid ul-Fitr: Friday, 20 March 2026
- Eid ul-Adha: Wednesday, 27 May 2026
- Raksha Bandhan: Friday, 28 August 2026
- Guru Nanak Jayanti: Tuesday, 24 November 2026

Interns are entitled to the fixed holidays of their office but are not eligible for optional holidays.

## 4. Working on a holiday
Employees who work on a fixed holiday with prior manager approval earn 1 compensatory off, as described in the Leave Policy for Employees.
