# Code of Conduct

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Legal and Compliance, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All employees, interns and contractors.

## 1. Acknowledgement
Every employee must read and acknowledge this Code of Conduct on KestrelHub within 7 days of joining and again every year in January.

## 2. Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to Compliance (compliance@kestrellabs.example), for example a family member who works for a vendor or competitor.

## 3. Gifts
- Employees may accept gifts from customers or vendors worth up to ₹2,000 per occasion.
- Gifts worth more than ₹2,000 must be declared to Compliance within 7 days and may have to be returned.
- Cash, gift cards and vouchers must never be accepted, whatever their value.

## 4. Outside employment
- Outside employment, paid freelancing or consulting requires prior written approval from HR.
- Working for a competitor, in any capacity, is not allowed.

## 5. Social media
Employees must not share confidential information on social media or speak on behalf of Kestrel Labs without approval from the communications team.

## 6. Dress code
The dress code is smart casual. Fridays are casual. Formal business attire is required for client meetings.

## 7. Reporting concerns
- Concerns about unethical behavior can be reported to ethics@kestrellabs.example or through the anonymous ethics hotline.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
- Complaints of sexual harassment are handled under the POSH Policy.

## 8. Disciplinary process
- Breaches are handled in stages: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, and termination.
- Serious misconduct, such as fraud, theft, violence or harassment, can lead to immediate termination without going through the earlier stages.
