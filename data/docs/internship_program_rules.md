# Internship Program Rules

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources (Campus and Internships), Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All interns at Kestrel Labs.

## 1. Duration
- Internships last a minimum of 2 months and a maximum of 6 months.
- An internship can be extended once, as long as the total duration does not exceed 6 months.

## 2. Stipend
- Undergraduate interns: ₹30,000 per month.
- Postgraduate interns: ₹40,000 per month.
- The stipend is paid on the last working day of each month, as described in the Payroll Policy. Interns are not eligible for PF, bonuses or salary advances.

## 3. Working hours and location
Interns follow the working hours in the Attendance and Working Hours Policy. Interns must work from the office at least 4 days per week, as set by the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

## 4. Mentor and reviews
- Every intern is assigned a mentor from the hiring team.
- Interns have a mid-term review halfway through the internship and a final review in the last week.

## 5. Systems and devices
- Interns receive a company laptop. Personal devices may not be used for company work.
- Interns do not get access to production systems or customer data. See the IT Access Policy.

## 6. Certificate
An internship certificate is issued within 15 days of the last day, provided the final review is completed and the laptop and ID card are returned.

## 7. Pre-placement offer (PPO)
Interns who complete at least 3 months and receive an "Exceeds expectations" rating in the final review are considered for a pre-placement offer. A PPO is not guaranteed.

## 8. Benefits
Interns are not covered by the group health insurance policy. They are covered only by the group personal accident policy. See the Health Insurance Policy.

## 9. Early termination
Either the intern or Kestrel Labs may end the internship early with 7 days of written notice. Kestrel Labs may end an internship immediately for serious misconduct.

## 10. Leave
Leave rules for interns are set out in the Intern Leave Policy.
