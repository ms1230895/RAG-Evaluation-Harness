# Resignation and Exit Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees. Interns follow the early termination rules in the Internship Program Rules.

## 1. Notice period
- Employees on probation: 15 days.
- Confirmed employees in bands B1 to B4: 60 days.
- Confirmed employees in bands B5 and B6: 90 days.

The notice period starts on the day the resignation is submitted on KestrelHub.

## 2. Notice period buyout
An employee may buy out all or part of the remaining notice period, with approval from the reporting manager and HR. The buyout amount is calculated on basic salary for the days not served and is deducted from the full and final settlement.

## 3. Leave during notice period
- Earned leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period.
- Casual leave and sick leave can be taken during the notice period with manager approval.

## 4. Exit process
- An exit interview with HR is scheduled in the last 2 weeks of the notice period.
- The laptop, ID card and all other company property must be returned on or before the last working day.
- All accounts are deactivated by 6:00 PM on the last working day, as set out in the IT Access Policy.

## 5. Full and final settlement
- The full and final (F&F) settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day.
- It includes salary for the days worked, encashment of earned leave up to a maximum of 30 days, and gratuity for employees with 5 or more years of continuous service.
- It deducts any notice period buyout, unrecovered salary advance, unsettled travel advance, and the value of unreturned company property.

## 6. Letters
The relieving letter and experience letter are issued on the last working day, provided that exit clearance is complete.

## 7. Rehiring
Former employees who resigned and served their notice period are eligible to be rehired 6 months after their last working day.
