# Business Travel Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Finance and Administration, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees traveling on company business. Interns may travel only with written manager approval and follow band B1 entitlements.

## 1. Approval and booking
- All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager on KestrelHub before booking.
- Flights, trains and hotels are booked by the company travel desk (travel@kestrellabs.example).
- Domestic flights must be requested at least 7 days before the travel date. Requests made later need department head approval.

## 2. Class of travel
- Domestic flights: economy class for all bands.
- International flights: economy class, except that employees in band B5 and above may fly business class on flights longer than 8 hours.
- Trains: AC 2-tier for all bands.

## 3. Hotel limits (per night, excluding taxes)
- Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata): up to ₹6,000
- Tier 2 and other cities: up to ₹4,000
- International: up to USD 180

## 4. Per diem
- Domestic travel: ₹1,200 per day, covering meals and incidental expenses.
- International travel: USD 60 per day.
- Per diem is paid for each day away from the home city, including the day of departure and the day of return. When per diem is paid, meal bills cannot be claimed separately under the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 5. Local conveyance
- Cabs to and from airports and client sites: reimbursed at actuals with a receipt.
- Use of a personal car for business: ₹12 per km. Use of a personal two-wheeler: ₹5 per km.

## 6. Travel advance
Employees can request a travel advance of up to 75% of the estimated trip cost. The advance must be settled with receipts within 15 days of returning from the trip. Unsettled advances are recovered from the next salary.

## 7. International travel
International travel requires approval from the department head and a valid visa arranged through the travel desk. Employees must carry the company travel insurance card for every international trip.
