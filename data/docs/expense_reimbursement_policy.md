# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Finance, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees. Interns may claim only expenses pre-approved by their mentor.

## 1. Submitting a claim
- All claims are submitted on KestrelHub with a scanned copy of the original receipt or tax invoice.
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless approved by the department head.
- Claims approved by the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th are paid in the next payroll.

## 2. Approval limits
- Claims up to ₹2,000: approved automatically after a finance check of the receipt.
- Claims above ₹2,000 and up to ₹25,000: require approval from the reporting manager.
- Claims above ₹25,000: require approval from the reporting manager and the department head.

## 3. Monthly allowances
- Internet: employees on hybrid or remote work can claim up to ₹1,000 per month for home internet, against the monthly bill.
- Mobile: employees in band B3 and above can claim up to ₹500 per month for mobile phone bills.

## 4. Client entertainment
Client entertainment is reimbursed up to ₹1,500 per person per occasion, including the employee. The names of the clients and the business purpose must be recorded in the claim.

## 5. Missing receipts
If a receipt is lost, the employee may submit a self-declaration for amounts up to ₹500 per claim, at most twice per quarter. Larger claims without a receipt are not reimbursed.

## 6. Not reimbursable
- Alcohol and tobacco
- Traffic fines, parking fines and penalties
- Personal entertainment, personal grooming or personal shopping
- Expenses for family members or friends
- Any expense already paid for by the travel desk or covered by a per diem

## 7. Business travel
Travel bookings, hotel limits and per diem rates are set by the Travel Policy. Travel-related claims (local cab fares, mileage, excess baggage) are submitted through this reimbursement process.

## 8. False claims
Submitting a false or inflated claim is a breach of the Code of Conduct and can lead to termination.
