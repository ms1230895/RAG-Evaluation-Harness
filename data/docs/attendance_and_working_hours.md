# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees and interns.

## 1. Working week
The working week is Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs.

## 2. Working hours
- The standard working day is 9 hours, including a 1-hour lunch break.
- Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. All employees must be available during core hours.
- Employees may start work at any time between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM.

## 3. Recording attendance
- In the office, attendance is recorded by the biometric scanner at the entrance.
- When working from home, employees check in and check out on KestrelHub.
- If an employee misses check-in 3 or more times in a month, the reporting manager is notified automatically.

## 4. Half day
A working day with less than 4.5 hours of recorded work is marked as a half day. The other half is deducted from casual leave, or treated as leave without pay if no casual leave is available.

## 5. Weekend work and overtime
- Employees in bands B1 and B2 who work on a weekend with prior manager approval earn 1 compensatory off for each full day worked.
- Employees in band B3 and above are not paid overtime and do not earn compensatory off for weekend work.

## 6. Night shifts
Employees in the customer support team who work night shifts between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM receive a night shift allowance of ₹400 per night, paid with the monthly salary.

## 7. Hybrid and remote work
Rules on how many days must be worked from the office are in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.
