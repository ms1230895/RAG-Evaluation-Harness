# Leave Policy for Employees

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, Kestrel Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: All full-time employees (confirmed and on probation). Interns are covered by the separate Intern Leave Policy.

## 1. Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. All leave is applied for and approved on KestrelHub, the company HR portal. The reporting manager is the approver for all leave types listed here.

## 2. Casual leave (CL)
- Every full-time employee gets 8 days of casual leave per leave year.
- Casual leave is credited quarterly: 2 days on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October.
- A maximum of 3 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at one time.
- Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December. It cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.
- Employees who join mid-quarter receive casual leave for that quarter on a pro-rata basis, rounded down to the nearest half day.

## 3. Sick leave (SL)
- Every full-time employee gets 10 days of sick leave per leave year, credited in full on 1 January (pro-rata for new joiners).
- A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum accumulated balance of 30 days.
- Sick leave cannot be encashed, either during employment or at exit.

## 4. Earned leave (EL)
- Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service, which is 18 days per year.
- Earned leave starts accruing from the date of joining, but employees on probation cannot take earned leave until they are confirmed.
- Earned leave of 3 or more days must be applied for at least 14 calendar days in advance.
- Unused earned leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum accumulated balance of 45 days. Days above 45 lapse on 31 December.
- Earned leave is encashable only at exit, up to a maximum of 30 days, calculated on the last drawn basic salary. See the Resignation and Exit Policy.
- Weekends and public holidays that fall inside a continuous block of earned leave are not counted as leave days.

## 5. Other leave types
- Bereavement leave: 3 paid days on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law).
- Marriage leave: 5 paid days, available once during employment with Kestrel Labs. It must be taken within 3 months of the wedding date.
- Compensatory off: an employee who works on a public holiday with prior manager approval earns 1 compensatory off. It must be used within 60 days or it lapses.
- Leave without pay (LWP): may be granted by the manager and HR only after all casual, sick and earned leave balances are exhausted. LWP of more than 15 days in a leave year needs approval from the HR Business Partner.

## 6. Unapproved absence
Absence without approved leave is marked as LWP. Unapproved absence for more than 3 consecutive working days is treated as a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct.
